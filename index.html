Your entries live only in this browser, and browsers can clear stored data on their own — iPhones especially. Adding this to your Home Screen makes that less likely but cannot rule it out. A backup is the only copy that survives it.

On iPhone, always open it the same way. Safari and the Home Screen icon can keep separate copies of your entries, so a day logged in one may be missing from the other. If entries seem to have vanished, check the other one before assuming they are gone.

    Add this to your Home Screen. On iPhone: tap Share, then “Add to Home Screen”. On Android: your browser’s menu — it may be labelled “Install app”, though nothing is installed on your phone. You get an icon and your own window, and your entries are much less likely to be cleared by the browser — though not immune, so keep backups either way.

    Nothing is downloaded onto your device and nothing is granted access to it. This stays an ordinary web page in your browser — the shortcut only changes how you open it and how long your entries are kept.
